The personal side

Personal anecdote

I find that nowadays people rely and allow themselves to switch off their brain because they have the comfort of AI giving them responses in situations where otherwise I would argue using your brain is better. For example, certain classmates of mine have openly stated that they are comfortable using ChatGPT as a therapist. That means they confide their feelings in it and expect the chatbot to tell them what the best course of actions are based on what their feelings are.
